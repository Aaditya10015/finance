# Investment Recommendation System

## 1. Problem Statement

Many individuals find it difficult to understand their financial position and choose suitable investment options based on their income, savings, risk-taking ability, and investment duration. This project aims to develop a simple Python-based system that analyzes these factors and provides basic investment recommendations along with a financial health score and future investment projection.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project covers:

* Analysis of monthly income and expenditure
* Calculation of monthly surplus and savings rate
* Calculation of investment rate and available savings period
* Generation of a financial health score
* Investment recommendations based on risk and duration
* Suggested investment allocation
* Calculation of projected future investment value and estimated gain

The system is designed for basic educational and financial planning purposes.

## 3. Target Users

The system is mainly intended for:

* Students learning about personal finance
* Beginners interested in investment planning
* Individuals who want a basic overview of their financial position
* Users learning Python through practical projects

## 4. High-Level Features

* User financial information input
* Risk-taking ability selection
* Investment duration selection
* Financial health score generation
* Savings and investment analysis
* Risk-based investment recommendations
* Suggested investment allocation
* Future value calculation
* Final financial report generation
